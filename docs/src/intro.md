# Welcome to FicHub!

FicHub is a free, open-source way to **download and discover fanfiction** from
AO3, FanFiction.net, and more — converted into clean EPUB, HTML, or PDF files
you can read on any device, even offline.

## What you can do here

- **Download** stories from multiple sites as EPUB, HTML, PDF, TXT, and more
- **Search** with filters that go beyond what the source sites offer
- **Bookmark** favorites, **rate** stories, and **comment** with other readers
- **Discover** new reads through recommendations and the leaderboard
- **Level up** — earn XP, climb 100 levels, unlock features on the Ability Tree
- **Customize** — compose recipe blends, pick theme presets, tune your dashboard
- **Join the forum** — talk with other readers in categories/topics, follow
  threads, and search the discussion
