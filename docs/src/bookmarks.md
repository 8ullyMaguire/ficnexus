# Saving Your Favorites

Found a story you love? Save it so you never lose it. Bookmarks are tied to
your account, so you'll need to be logged in (see the [Quickstart](./quickstart.md)).

## How to Bookmark

1. **Log in** to your FicHub account (click "Login" in the top right).
2. Open a story — either from a search result or after downloading it.
3. Click the **♡ heart button**.
4. Add a note if you want (optional).
5. Click **Save**.

The story is now in your bookmarks.

## Viewing Your Bookmarks

Click the ** Bookmarks** link in the menu. From there you can:

- See when you saved each story
- Read your notes about each story
- Remove bookmarks you no longer want

## Making Bookmarks Private

When you add a bookmark, check the **Private** box. Only you will be able to
see it. (Private bookmarks also don't feed into public "for you" suggestions.)

## Removing a Bookmark

1. Go to your Bookmarks page.
2. Find the story you want to remove.
3. Click the **✕ remove** button.

## Why bookmark?

Bookmarks power FicHub's recommendations: the more you bookmark, the better
your "Recommended for you" row gets. See [Getting Recommendations](./recommendations.md).
