# Rating Stories

Let authors and other readers know what you think. Ratings are one of the
signals FicHub uses to recommend stories to you.

## How to Rate

When you visit a story's FicHub page, you'll see a **5-star rating widget**:

- 1 star — not for you
- 4 stars — really good
- 5 stars — loved it

Click a star to cast your rating. You can change it anytime — your latest
rating replaces the old one.

You can also write an **in-depth review**: a title, a few sentences about why
you recommend (or don't) the story, alongside your star rating. Reviews are a
richer signal for both other readers and the recommendation engine.

## How Ratings Work

- Every story shows its **average rating** and **how many people rated it**.
- FicHub keeps the public side **positive-only**: we show how many readers
  loved a story, not how many disliked it. This keeps the atmosphere friendly
  for authors while still giving you honest signal.
- Higher-rated stories are recommended more often.
- Your ratings help FicHub suggest better stories to *you*.

## Be Kind!

Real people write these stories for free. Be honest in your ratings, but
always be respectful. If a story isn't for you, a low star count is enough —
there's no need for harsh words.
