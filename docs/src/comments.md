# Chatting About Fics

Discuss stories with other readers. Comments are public and linked to your
username, so you'll need an account.

> Want a broader discussion? The [community forum](/forum) has categories
> and topics for talking about anything — stories, recs, the site, and more.

## How to Comment

1. Go to a story's FicHub page (click the title in search results).
2. Scroll down to the **Comments** section.
3. Type your comment (max 2,000 characters).
4. Press **Ctrl+Enter** or click **Post Comment**.

## What can you comment about?

- What you liked about the story
- Your favorite characters or moments
- Questions for other readers
- Theories about what happens next

## Community Rules

1. **Be kind** — no bullying, harassment, or hate speech.
2. **No spoilers without warning** — use common courtesy.
3. **Stay on topic** — talk about the story, not unrelated things.
4. **No spam** — don't post ads or unrelated links.

Violations may result in your comment being removed.

## Who can see my comments?

Your comments are public and linked to your username. If you want to comment
anonymously, you'd need to do that on the original site.
